About Us

Welcome to Sanjha Chulha, where the warmth of tradition meets the rich, authentic flavors of our heritage. Inspired by the communal earthen ovens of Punjab, our restaurant is a place where food brings people together just like a shared village hearth. We take pride in serving meticulously crafted dishes, using time-honored recipes and the freshest ingredients to create a truly soul-satisfying experience. Whether you’re joining us for a family feast or a quiet meal, we invite you to pull up a chair and become part of our story.
