Shahi Paneer

Creamy tomato-based curry. Shahi Paneer comes from the royal kitchens of the Mughal Empire, where rich and creamy dishes were made for kings. The word “Shahi” means royal, which reflects its luxurious ingredients like cream, nuts, and spices. Over time, it became a popular Punjabi dish enjoyed by everyone, not just royalty.

Saag

Traditional mustard greens dish. Saag is a traditional Punjabi dish made from leafy greens and has been eaten in rural villages for generations. It was commonly prepared during winter using fresh mustard leaves grown locally. The dish represents simple, healthy eating and is often served with makki di roti as a staple meal.

Naan

Soft bread cooked in a tandoor. Naan has its origins in Central and South Asia and became popular during the Mughal Empire period. It was traditionally cooked in a clay oven called a tandoor and served in royal courts. Today, naan is a common bread enjoyed worldwide with many different dishes.
